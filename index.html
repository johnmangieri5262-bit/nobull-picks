Competition Rules
  • Picks: Each player selects 3-10 investments (stocks, ETFs, or crypto).
  • Drafting Period: 1 month before the competition starts, you can begin adding picks (minimum 1). Add, remove, or change picks freely until lock date.
  • Lock Date: On competition start, all picks are frozen and entry prices are recorded at market close.
  • Scoring: Equal-weighted average % return across all picks. If you pick 5 stocks, each counts for 20% of your total return.
  • Quarterly Competitions: Each quarter (Q1-Q4) runs independently. Winners are crowned at quarter end.
  • Yearly Championship: Your best 3 quarterly performances are averaged for the yearly leaderboard.
  • Eligible Tickers: All US-listed stocks and ETFs. Top-50 crypto by market cap. No penny stocks under $1.
  • Tie Breaker: Player with fewer picks wins (more concentrated = more conviction).
  • Late Entry: Players can join mid-draft but must submit picks before lock date. No exceptions.
